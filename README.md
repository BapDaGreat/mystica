# Mystica (مِسْتِيكَا) — Luxury Stationery & Keepsake Atelier
**Multidisciplinary Brand Identity, Packaging Pre-Press, Motion & Full-Stack Website Pitch for Hold Media Agency**
**Designed & Developed by Dayle Angelo Peña**

---

## Brief Deliverables Covered

1. **Brand Identity System:**
   - Primary & Secondary Bilingual Lockups (`MYSTICA · مِسْتِيكَا`)
   - Signature 8-Pointed Celestial Star-Seal & Silk Ribbon Monogram (with interactive construction grid)
   - Tactile Material & Color Swatch Cabinet (`Deep Burgundy Buckram #4A121A`, `600gsm Warm Ivory Cotton Rag #F8F4EC`, `Antique Champagne Foil #C8A96E`, `Reserve Wine Velvet #320A10`, `Blush Silk Parchment #EFE7DA`)
   - Bilingual English (`Bodoni Moda` + `Jost`) & Arabic (`Amiri` + `Readex Pro`) Typographic Specimen Sheet on a shared optical baseline grid.
2. **Packaging, Stationery & Print-Ready Artwork:**
   - Studio mockups for the Solander Keepsake Box, Correspondence Flat-Lay, Milestone Drawer Coffret, and Ramadan/Eid Gifting Suite.
   - Interactive CAD Pre-Press Dieline Inspector (`240 × 180 × 45 mm`) with toggleable separation plates (`0.25pt Die-Cut`, `Crease/Score`, `3.0mm Bleed`, `Kurz Champagne Gold Foil Plate`, `0.45mm Blind Deboss`, and `16mm Silk Ribbon Slots`).
3. **Elegant, Intuitive Website (Desktop & Mobile) + Full Website Development:**
   - Live interactive **Bespoke Keepsake & Stationery Customizer** allowing clients to configure paper stock, silk ribbon weave, foil/letterpress finish, bilingual English/Arabic inscriptions, and edition quantity.
4. **Social Media Designs, Digital Ad Creatives & Motion Design:**
   - 9:16 Instagram Reels / TikTok vertical ad unit, 4:5 Meta bilingual GCC feed carousel card, and choreographed brand motion reel with interactive gallery light-catch physics.
