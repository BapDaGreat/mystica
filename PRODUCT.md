## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS in standalone `mystica/` directory (ready for GitHub Pages deployment).

## Users

1. **Primary Evaluators (Hold Media Agency Hiring & Creative Leadership):** Evaluating Dayle Angelo Peña for the Multidisciplinary Designer role to bring **Mystica** to life across brand identity, packaging/print dielines, web design + full-stack web development, digital ad creatives, motion design, and bilingual (English + Arabic) typography.
2. **Simulated End Customers (Mystica Luxury Clientele):** Discerning buyers in the GCC and globally seeking heirloom stationery, wax-sealed correspondence suites, silk-ribboned keepsake boxes, and bespoke celebratory paper goods for weddings, births, Ramadan/Eid gifting, and milestone anniversaries.

## Product Purpose

A dual-purpose flagship experience for **Mystica** ("Celebrating Life's Meaningful Moments"):
- **Live Flagship Storefront & Interactive Atelier:** Demonstrates an authentic, tactile luxury stationery and keepsake brand website for desktop and mobile—including an interactive Keepsake Suite & Ribbon/Foil Customizer, bilingual English/Arabic (`EN / العربية`) typographic live preview and switching, and curated seasonal/milestone collections.
- **Interactive Brand Identity, Packaging & Agency Pitch Dossier:** Allows Hold Media Agency reviewers to inspect the complete visual identity system (primary/secondary marks, bilingual Arabic/English logotype lockups, color & paper stock swatches, signature star-seal icon), realistic studio packaging mockups with inspectable print dielines (bleed, cut, crease, hot-foil stamp plates), animated social/digital ad creatives, motion design tokens, and explicit confirmation that Dayle Angelo Peña delivers full end-to-end website development alongside brand, print, and motion design.

## Positioning

Unlike generic template portfolios or static PDF brand decks, this pitch ships the living Mystica product ecosystem itself: tactile cotton-paper and deep burgundy (`#4A121A` / `#320A10`) material physicality, museum-grade bilingual Latin + Arabic calligraphy/typesetting, interactive foil & silk-ribbon bespoke customization, inspectable print-ready packaging dielines, and choreographed motion—proving in a single URL that one multidisciplinary designer-developer can take Hold Media Agency's brief from concept to print press and production code.
